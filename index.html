how to play

The goal

Knock everyone off Cloud Island into the water. The last fighter still on the island wins the round. Win enough rounds and you win the whole fight and get the crown.

Team vs team: pick "Team vs team" for the game mode, then put each fighter on the Red team or the Blue team. You can't punch or grab your own teammates. A team wins the round when everyone on the other team is in the water.

Racing: pick "Racing" for the game mode and everyone drives a kart 3 laps around a race track. Pick the Grand Prix, or the Space Race, where you drive on roads between planets (don't fall off into space!). Hold jump to go, down to brake, and steer with left and right. Items lie at random spots all around the road in glowing yellow rings, so you can see what you'll get. Drive over one to pick it up (you can hold one at a time), then press punch to use it: a speed boost, a banana peel to drop behind you, a green bouncy shell, or a red chaser shell that flies at whoever is just ahead of you. Stay on the road, because the grass slows you right down. The first one over the finish line wins.

Horror: a monster is looking for you in a dark, spooky house! Move all around with left, right, up (jump) and down. Press punch next to a bed, closet, box or other furniture to hide in it, and press it again to come out. The monster sees in a cone in front of it (yellow, or red when it's chasing someone), and your heart beats faster when it's close. Don't let it see you hide, or it will come and pull you out! You can't stay hidden forever, either: you get more and more scared until you jump out screaming. Survive for 60 seconds and you win. Racing and Horror work online too.

A fight, step by step

  1. Press OK, then pick who plays each fighter on the "who is with who" screen.
  2. Press "make fight happen" and wait for 3, 2, 1, FIGHT!
  3. Punch someone to knock down their health. Everyone has 200 hp. When it runs out, stars spin over their head and they are dizzy and can't move.
  4. Grab the dizzy fighter. Walk them to the edge of the island.
  5. Press grab again to throw them into the water. Splash, they're out!

The fighters

Maps

Cloud Island: a floating island over the water with three clouds to stand on. The middle cloud moves.

Lava Rocks: black rocks in a sea of lava. Falling in the lava burns your hp every second, so jump out fast! If your hp runs out in the lava, you sink and you're out. Between the rocks, lava geysers blast up on a timer. Watch the stopwatches: when the lava bubbles and the clock flashes red, get out of the way.

Roadside Fights: three floors. Cars zoom along the road at the bottom (HONK! means one is coming, and it hits for 50). Birds swoop across the sidewalk in the middle (tweet tweet, 15). The power lines at the top zap anyone standing on them (bzzz then ZAAP, 5). Jump to dodge cars, and press down to drop off a wire before it zaps.

Snowy Hills: two icy cliffs with a broken bridge between them. The ice is slippery, so it's hard to stop! When a yellow caution sign flashes and beeps, ice is about to fall on the left side or snow on the right. Watch for the red warning mark on the ground and get out of the way.

Playing online

  1. One person clicks "play online", types their name and clicks "host".
  2. The host sends the 4-letter code to friends.
  3. Friends click "play online", type the code and click "join".
  4. Everyone clicks a fighter. The host can add computer fighters, kick players and set the rounds.
  5. The host clicks "make fight happen". Keep the game window open and in front while you play.

Tips

  • Special moves: Shocked Circle rolls into people (35 damage). Yelling Square jumps up and slams down on anyone close by (45). Angry Triangle flips into the air and lands with spike waves that shoot along the ground both ways (30 each). Goofy Sparkle goes on a sugar rush: he zooms all around the closest fighter in a wild yellow scribble and hits them 4 times (10 each). Grumpy Shuriken dashes straight forward, spinning, and hits everyone in his way (30 each). Once you free him, Missing Arrow makes glitch blocks fall from the sky in front of him (50 to everyone they land on). The Admin (from the codes page) sends out a giant "Banned" sign that zooms across the whole map and back: it does 100000000 damage, so anyone it touches is out! If they miss, they do a silly flip or a dusty crash instead.
  • Got grabbed? Mash punch, jump or grab to wriggle free.
  • Knocked off the edge? Use your double jump and steer back to the island.
  • Throwing a dizzy fighter sends them much farther than throwing an awake one.
  • Hide on the clouds, but watch out: the middle one moves.
  • Yelling Square is heavy, so it's hard to throw. Goofy Sparkle flies far.
  • Two people can share one keyboard: one on WASD, one on the arrows.
