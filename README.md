# Fight for Fun

A Party Animals–style brawler designed by Mason, with Alex and Mason's dad.

Nine shape fighters (and a secret tenth one you have to rescue) punch, grab and throw each other off Cloud Island. The last one standing wins the round.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a web browser, or play the GitHub Pages version.

## Special moves

- **Shocked Circle** rolls into people (35 damage). If it misses, it does a flip.
- **Angry Triangle** flips into the air and lands with spike waves that shoot along the ground both ways (30 damage each).
- **Yelling Square** jumps up and slams down on anyone close by (45 damage). If it misses, it crashes into a puff of dust.
- **Goofy Sparkle** goes on a **sugar rush**: he zooms all around the closest fighter in a wild yellow scribble and hits them 4 times (10 damage each, 40 in all). The last hit knocks them away.
- **Grumpy Shuriken** dashes straight forward, spinning like a real shuriken, and hits everyone in his way (30 damage each). Careful: he can dash right off a cliff!
- **Missing Arrow** (the secret fighter) does a **glitch fest**: he glitches out, and a big pile of red glitch blocks and red and blue scribbles falls from the sky in front of him (a dashed red ring shows where). Everyone it lands on takes 50 damage and goes flying. It's a big one, so he has to wait 6 seconds before he can do it again.

Every fighter has 200 hp. When it runs out, they get dizzy and can be thrown.

## Game modes

- **Free for all:** everyone for themselves. The last fighter on the island wins the round.
- **Team vs team:** Red team against Blue team. You can't punch or grab your teammates. A team wins the round when the whole other team is in the water.
- **Racing:** like Mario Kart! Everyone drives a kart 3 laps around a track. Pick the track under the map picture:
  - **Grand Prix:** a twisty track with a grandstand full of cheering fans in the middle. Stay on the road, because the grass slows you right down.
  - **Space Race** (drawn by Mason): grey roads in outer space between Earth, a tan planet with a big red spot, and a stripy blue planet. You can drive all over the planets, but if you drive off a road you float away into space and come back where you fell off.

  Hold jump to go, down to brake, and steer with left and right. Items lie on the road in glowing yellow rings, so you can see what you'll get. Drive over one to pick it up (you can hold one at a time), then press punch to use it:
  - **Speed boost:** a burst of speed.
  - **Banana peel:** drops behind you. Whoever drives over it spins out.
  - **Bouncy shell (green):** shoots straight ahead and bounces off the walls.
  - **Chaser shell (red):** flies after whoever is just ahead of you.

  Items are scattered at random spots all around the track, different every race, and 3 seconds after one is picked up a new random item shows up somewhere else. The first one over the finish line wins, and once they finish, everyone else has 15 seconds to cross the line. Racing works online too: the host picks Racing and a track in the team lobby.
- **Horror:** a monster is looking for you in a dark, spooky house with six rooms! It's dark everywhere except near you and the candles, and lightning flashes now and then. Move all around with left, right, up (jump) and down. Press punch next to a bed, closet, box, couch, fridge, barrel, bookshelf, chest or bathtub to hide in it, and again to come out. The monster comes out of the basement after 5 seconds and walks from room to room. It sees in a cone in front of it (yellow, or red when it's chasing someone), and it can hear you if you're right next to it. When it gets close, your heart starts beating faster.
  - If it sees you hide, it comes and pulls you out ("FOUND YOU!"). Sometimes it sniffs around and checks hiding spots it walks past.
  - You can't stay hidden forever. The scared meter over your hiding spot fills up, and when it's full you jump out screaming "AAAH!"
  - If it catches you, you're out. Survive for 60 seconds and you win. If it catches everyone, the monster wins.

  Horror works online too, and on one computer you can play it alone against the monster.

## Maps

- **Cloud Island:** a floating island over the water, with three clouds to stand on (the middle one moves).
- **Snowy Hills:** two slippery icy cliffs with a broken bridge between them. Caution signs flash and beep before ice falls on the left side or snow on the right.
- **Roadside Fights:** three floors. Cars on the road (50), birds on the sidewalk (15) and zapping power lines on top (5), each with its own caution sign and warning sound.
- **Lava Rocks:** black rocks in a lava sea. Lava burns your hp every second (jump out!), and lava geysers blast up between the rocks on a timer.

## Shop

Every fight you finish earns 10 coins, and 15 more if a person (not the computer) wins it. Spend coins in the **shop** on the main menu.

The shop is a room you walk around in. Walk your fighter up to a stand and press punch to buy what's on it, or punch again to put it on or take it off. You can also take things off from anywhere in the shop with the ✕ buttons at the bottom. To see the whole shop, close the pop-up at the bottom with its ✕ in the corner, and click **show info** to bring it back (punch still works while it's closed). Use the arrows at the top to pick which fighter you're shopping as. The shop has two floors. Downstairs is the fighty shop. Take the stairs on the left (walk up and press punch) to reach the **funny fun shop** on the 2nd floor, where things go when downstairs runs out of room. Its shopkeeper is very shy, but she'll chat if you talk to her.

A "staff only" door in the corner of the funny fun shop goes into the **break room**, where both shopkeepers take their break. The fighty shopkeeper sits on the couch with a mug of coffee and the shy shopkeeper has her tea. Talk to them and they take turns. The longer you stay, the more stuff they give you. Every 15 seconds you get coins (1 at a time at first, then more the longer you stay, up to 5). At 30 seconds the shy shopkeeper gives you a **pink bow**, at 1 minute the fighty shopkeeper gives you a **staff badge**, and at 2 minutes you get a big 20-coin bonus. The chalkboard shows your break time and what's coming next. If you leave, the timer starts over. Put the bow and badge on or take them off with the buttons at the bottom of the shop.

A ladder in the funny fun shop (with a "keep out!" sign) goes up to the dark, dusty 3rd floor, where glowing eyes watch you from the shadows and vanish when you get close. Ask either shopkeeper about the 3rd floor and they get very scared.

An elevator in the attic goes up to the **manager's office** on the 4th floor. A big spinny chair faces away from you behind the desk, and when you walk close it spins around to show the manager, **%#@%**. He doesn't like visitors: a moment later he puts you through a **boss fight** on a glitched-out Cloud Island, with a dark sky full of flickering red, blue and purple glitch blocks (beat him for 50 coins).

After the fight, the **chase** starts. You're in a long, dark, glitched-out world: a glowing grid, flickering floor tiles, broken chunks of pixels and error messages floating in the void. %#@% climbs out of a glitchy hole in the floor: a tall grey glitch man with a round black head, a big grey hand over his face, red arrows for arms and red, blue and purple glitch blocks all over him (drawn from Mason's picture). Everything glitches, and every few seconds he glitches forward. Run right, jump over the glitch cubes (he floats through them), and dive into the glitchy rift at the end to get back to the 1st floor safe. If he catches you, he throws you out of the shop.

**Sneak past the boss:** there's a secret way out of the office. Once %#@% spins around, you have a few seconds before the boss fight starts. Run past his desk and keep walking, right off the left edge of the screen, to get away to the secret **5th floor**, drawn by Mason. It's pitch black up there, with %#@%'s red and blue glitch blocks all over the walls (they glitch sideways now and then) and a poster: **HAVE YOU SEEN HIM? MISSING**, with an arrow pointing at it. Who is missing...? Jump into the big portal in the middle to find out.

**The cage room and a secret fighter:** the 5th floor's portal takes you to the **cage room** (drawn by Mason). "Once you enter, you can't get out..." The missing guy from the poster, **Missing Arrow**, is locked in a cage on the left, and a creepy glitchy note says "H1 1F Y0U S3E TH1S, Y0U CANN0T B3 SAV3D...". There's a padlock on the cage, and to open it you play **Pop the Lock**: walk up to the cage and press punch to start. A red needle spins around the lock. Press punch right when it's on the yellow dot! Each pop counts down, turns the needle around and makes it a little faster. Pop it 12 times to open the lock. If the needle goes past the dot, it just comes around again, but if you press at the wrong time, the lock starts over from 12 and you lose a heart. Lose all 3 hearts and you get zapped out to the 1st floor. Pop the lock and Missing Arrow is free ("YOU SAVED ME!"): he becomes a **new fighter** you can play, and the portal on the right opens to take you home. The shopkeepers have never seen him before, so if you shop as Missing Arrow they act like he's a total stranger ("W-who are you?! I've never seen you before..."). Being locked up left him scared of everything (wide eyes, worried brows, a wobbly mouth and a sweat drop, and he trembles) and really glitchy: he always has a faint red and blue fringe, and he glitches all the time. He jumps around, red and blue copies of him split apart, glitch squares flicker around him, and strips of him slide sideways with scan lines and static, like a broken TV. Until then, he shows up as a locked "???" on the "who is with who" screen. (He isn't in online games yet.)

To talk to the grey shopkeeper, walk up to the counter and press punch. Each time you press it, they say something new: tips, how many coins you need, and a joke.

- **Sunglasses (20 coins):** dark shades with a shine. Every fighter can wear them except Cool Star, who already has its own.
- **Cool hat (25 coins):** a tilted black top hat.
- **Super cape (30 coins):** a bright red cape that flutters behind you.
- **Bat wings (35 coins, LIMITED!):** spooky dark purple bat wings that flap behind you. They're only for sale at Halloween (all of October, or whenever Halloween is set to Always in settings), on a stand in the funny fun shop on the 2nd floor. The rest of the year the stand says "Halloween only". If you buy them, they're yours to keep and wear all year.
- **Skins:** new colours for your fighters, at the skins booth (a big gold mirror) in the funny fun shop on the 2nd floor. The mirror shows your fighter in each skin. Every fighter has their own skins, so pick a fighter with the arrows at the top, then click a skin on the card to buy it or put it on. Press punch at the mirror to switch between the skins that fighter has.
  - **Classic** (free): the fighter's normal colour.
  - **Ice** (25 coins): light icy blue with little frost spots.
  - **Shadow** (30 coins): dark and sneaky.
  - **Gold** (40 coins): shiny gold with a twinkle.
  - **Rainbow** (60 coins): changes colour all the time.
  - **Pumpkin** (35 coins, LIMITED!): orange with pumpkin stripes. Only for sale at Halloween, like the bat wings.

  Skins show up in fights, races and horror games on your computer (not in online games yet).
- **Glow goggles (free!):** up on the dark 3rd floor. Take them and the eyes and goggles turn red, the eyes shout "GET OUT!!!", push you back down the stairs and take the goggles back. Nobody gets to keep them.

**Easter egg:** 1 time in 10 when you open the shop, the YouTuber **SMI7Y** (a squishy bag of milk with headphones) is hanging out in front of the counter on the 1st floor. Walk up and press punch to say hi, and SMI7Y gives you their **SMI7Y headphones** (gaming headphones with a mic) for free. They aren't on a stand, so put them on or take them off with the buttons at the bottom of the shop. To always see SMI7Y, add `?smi7y` to the end of the game's address.

Coins and things you buy are saved in your browser. They show up in fights on your own computer, but not in online fights yet.

## Halloween

All through October the game gets spooky: a purple night sky with a moon, stars and bats, jack-o'-lanterns on the hills, cobwebs and pumpkins in the shop, and spooky menu music. Change it under **Halloween** in settings: In October, Always or Off.

## Christmas

All through December snow falls on every map, the menu hills turn snowy with Christmas trees, the shop gets twinkly lights, a wreath and Christmas trees, and the menu music turns jingly with sleigh bells. Change it under **Christmas** in settings: In December, Always or Off. If both themes are set to Always, Halloween wins.

## Play online

1. Click **play online**, type your name and click **host**.
2. Send your friends the 4-letter code.
3. They click **play online**, type the code and click **join**.
4. Everyone picks a fighter, and the host clicks **make fight happen**.

The host picks the game mode in the team lobby: Free for all, Team vs team, Racing (with a track picker) or Horror.

The host's browser runs the fight, and everyone else connects to it directly using [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com/). Keep the host's game window open and in front while you play. Some school or work networks block these direct connections.

## Controls

| | Keys: WASD | Keys: Arrows | Controller |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D | ← / → | Stick or D-pad |
| Jump (double jump in air) | W | ↑ | A |
| Drop through a cloud | S | ↓ | Stick down |
| Punch | F | K | X |
| Grab, then throw | G, G | L, L | B, B |
| Special move | H | ; | Y |
| Pause | Esc | Esc | Start |

You can change the keyboard keys on the **controls** page in the main menu.
