# Fight for Fun

A Party Animals–style brawler designed by Mason, with Alex and Mason's dad.

Nine shape fighters (and a secret tenth one you have to rescue) punch, grab and throw each other off Cloud Island. The last one standing wins the round.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a web browser, or play the GitHub Pages version.

## Special moves

- **Cool Star** does a **star shot** (from Mason's drawing): a spinning yellow star zooms straight forward and hits the first fighter it touches (40 damage). Cool Star can keep fighting while it flies.
- **Hexstar 2.0** has a **laser** (from Mason's drawing). Every punch she lands puts 10 energy in the blue tank next to her (up to 100, and it blinks when it's full). Her special shoots a laser beam straight across that uses up all the energy and hits everyone in its way: 1 damage for each bit of energy, so a full tank does 100. Then she has to wait 5 seconds.
- **Lovely Heart** has a **passive** instead (from Mason's drawing): **lifesteal**. Every punch that lands has a 25% chance to heal Lovely Heart 30 health (a green +30 pops up).
- **Shocked Circle** rolls into people (35 damage). If it misses, it does a flip.
- **Angry Triangle** flips into the air and lands with spike waves that shoot along the ground both ways (30 damage each).
- **Yelling Square** jumps up and slams down on anyone close by (45 damage). If it misses, it crashes into a puff of dust.
- **Goofy Sparkle** goes on a **sugar rush**: he zooms all around the closest fighter in a wild yellow scribble and hits them 4 times (10 damage each, 40 in all). The last hit knocks them away.
- **Grumpy Shuriken** dashes straight forward, spinning like a real shuriken, and hits everyone in his way (30 damage each). Careful: he can dash right off a cliff!
- **Missing Arrow** (the secret fighter) does a **glitch fest**: he glitches out, and a big pile of red glitch blocks and red and blue scribbles falls from the sky in front of him (a dashed red ring shows where). Everyone it lands on takes 50 damage and goes flying. It's a big one, so he has to wait 6 seconds before he can do it again.
- **Dark Bolt** (the secret fighter from chapter 48, from Mason's drawing) does a **shock wave**: a jagged yellow ring of lightning bursts out all around him and zaps everyone close by (32 damage each), knocking them away.

Every fighter has 200 hp. When it runs out, they get dizzy and can be thrown.

**Bigger maps for more fighters:** with 5 or more fighters, the map gets bigger: the islands, bridges and ledges stretch out sideways (and the gaps between them too), and the camera zooms out so you can still see it all. 4 fighters or less is normal size, and 10 fighters is about 1.45 times as wide. It works on every map, and online too.

The computer fighters are **aggressive**: they pick who to chase quickly, punch more often, hop around and never stand still for long, jump up after you, and use their specials a lot (even more on Hard). Cool Star fires star shots from far away, and Hexstar 2.0 fires her laser once her tank has some energy.

## Game modes

- **Free for all:** everyone for themselves. The last fighter on the island wins the round.
- **Team vs team:** Red team against Blue team. You can't punch or grab your teammates. A team wins the round when the whole other team is in the water.
- **Racing:** like Mario Kart! Everyone drives a kart 3 laps around a track. Pick the track under the map picture:
  - **Grand Prix:** a twisty track with a grandstand full of cheering fans in the middle. Stay on the road, because the grass slows you right down.
  - **Space Race** (drawn by Mason): grey roads in outer space between Earth, a tan planet with a big red spot, and a stripy blue planet. You can drive all over the planets, but if you drive off a road you float away into space and come back where you fell off.

  Hold jump to go, down to brake, and steer with left and right. Items lie on the road in glowing yellow rings, so you can see what you'll get. Drive over one to pick it up (you can hold one at a time), then press punch to use it:
  - **Speed boost:** a burst of speed.
  - **Banana peel:** drops behind you. Whoever drives over it spins out.
  - **Bouncy shell (green):** shoots straight ahead and bounces off the walls.
  - **Chaser shell (red):** flies after whoever is just ahead of you.

  Items are scattered at random spots all around the track, different every race, and 3 seconds after one is picked up a new random item shows up somewhere else. The first one over the finish line wins, and once they finish, everyone else has 15 seconds to cross the line. Racing works online too: the host picks Racing and a track in the team lobby.
- **Horror:** a monster is looking for you in a dark, spooky house with six rooms! It's dark everywhere except near you and the candles, and lightning flashes now and then. Move all around with left, right, up (jump) and down. Press punch next to a bed, closet, box, couch, fridge, barrel, bookshelf, chest or bathtub to hide in it, and again to come out. The monster comes out of the basement after 5 seconds and walks from room to room. It sees in a cone in front of it (yellow, or red when it's chasing someone), and it can hear you if you're right next to it. When it gets close, your heart starts beating faster.
  - If it sees you hide, it comes and pulls you out ("FOUND YOU!"). Sometimes it sniffs around and checks hiding spots it walks past.
  - You can't stay hidden forever. The scared meter over your hiding spot fills up, and when it's full you jump out screaming "AAAH!"
  - If it catches you, you're out. Survive for 60 seconds and you win. If it catches everyone, the monster wins.
  - Pick the map: the **Spooky house** or the **Spooky woods**. The woods are a dark forest with a fence all around, a dirt path, a campfire and lanterns. The trees block your way, and the monster can't see through them. It's foggy too, so it can't see as far as in the house. Hide in bushes (with red berries), hollow stumps, logs, a tent or behind a rock. The monster comes out of a cave on the right.
  - **Who is the monster?** The host picks: the computer, or one of the fighters (on the setup screen, or in the online team lobby). That person plays the monster instead of hiding: walk around, catch anyone you bump into, and press punch next to a hiding spot to check it ("FOUND YOU!" or "nobody here..."). It's dark for the monster too, except right around it, and the monster player can't see anyone else's light. On their screen, people are completely invisible unless they're in the light: close to the monster, in its vision cone, next to a candle or lantern, or in a lightning flash. So they really have to hunt in the dark. If the monster catches everyone, they win.

  Horror works online too, and on one computer you can play it alone against the monster.

- **K vs S (Killer vs Survivors), in real 3D!** A Killer hunts the Survivors through dark 3D woods with trees, rocks, little houses and real shadows. The camera is right behind your fighter. It's our own game, but like Forsaken on Roblox.
  - **Everyone is 3D.** Each fighter is their own shape (star, triangle, circle, heart...) puffed up into 3D, with their real face on the front, plus any sunglasses, hats and skins from the shop. The Admin is made of black blocks, with "me is admin / mason,dad / alex" on his head and "i ♥ forsken" on his body, like Mason drew him.
  - **Profile cards:** on the setup screen, under the picture, there's a card for the Killer and every Survivor you picked: their picture, health, speed, what they say, and what their abilities do (with the keys).
  - **The Survivors** are the fighters you pick. Walk like in Forsaken: up, left, down and right (W A S D) walk forward, left, back and right, and you look around with the **mouse**. Move the mouse over the game to look around, and push it to the left or right edge to keep turning. (In browsers that allow it, you can also click the game to grab the mouse, and Esc lets it go.) Until you move the mouse, left and right turn you instead. Hold grab to run (the blue bar is your stamina). Hold punch next to a generator to fix it. Press punch next to the Killer to **shove** it. Heavier fighters have more health, and faster fighters run a bit faster. Getting hit gives you a quick burst of speed to get away.
  - **Every Survivor has their own ability** (press special): Cool Star **Star Shot** (stuns the Killer), Angry Triangle **Spike Trap** (the Killer can't see it), Shocked Circle **Roll**, Sly Diamond **Sneaky** (see-through, no marks), Yelling Square **YELL!** (stuns the Killer, but it hears you), Goofy Sparkle **Sugar Rush**, Hexstar 2.0 **Fix-it Laser** (fixes a quarter of a generator), Lovely Heart **Healing Hug** (heals everyone close), Grumpy Shuriken **Shuriken Throw** (slows the Killer), Missing Arrow **Glitch**, Flower Girl **Flower Burst** (the Killer can't see), the Admin **Can't Catch Me** (a shield), and the shopkeepers' **Pop-up Ad** and **Shy Hide**. The boxes at the bottom of the screen show your keys and how long until you can use them again.
  - Fix **3 generators** and the lights come on: then you only have to last 20 more seconds. Or stay alive until the 2:30 timer runs out. With more Survivors, each generator takes longer to fix. Survivors still in at the end win. If the Killer catches everyone, the Killer wins.
  - **Terror radius:** when the Killer gets close you hear a heartbeat (faster the closer it is), the edges of the screen go red, and when it's right on you the chase music starts.
  - **How the Killer finds you:** running leaves red **scratch marks** on the ground, fixing a generator makes **noise** every few seconds (🔧), and running near the Killer is loud (👣). Only the Killer sees the marks and noises (an arrow shows noises that are behind it). The computer Killer follows the clues too, and goes to where it last saw someone.
  - **The Killer's hit** is a big lunge forward. It has four powers: grab, special, back + grab, and back + special.
  - **The Admin (Killer):** a giant version of the Admin, glowing dark red, with glitch cubes buzzing around him and a giant **BAN hammer**. **BAN!** (a red beam that freezes you for 2.5 seconds), **LAG** (glitches forward), **KICK** (charges forward, anyone in the way goes flying) and **SERVER SHUTDOWN** (every generator loses some of its fixing, and he sees everyone for 3 seconds).
  - **IT (Killer):** IT's tall, thin husk, like in the story, with dry pale skin, ribs, rags, hollow eyes glowing green-white and green light coming out of a crack. **Hungry Hands** (a hand bursts out of the ground under the closest Survivor), **I SEE YOU** (sees everyone through the trees and moves faster), **CRAWL** (drops down and crawls super fast) and **HOLLOW SCREAM** (everyone close is slowed down).
  - On the setup screen, pick the Killer (**random**, **the Admin** or **IT**), and whether you're **a Survivor** or **the Killer**. To be the Killer you need at least 2 fighters: you, and someone to hunt.
  - K vs S is only on one computer for now (not online). It needs the internet the first time you play, to load the 3D (it uses three.js).

## Maps

- **Cloud Island:** a floating island over the water, with three clouds to stand on (the middle one moves).
- **Snowy Hills:** two slippery icy cliffs with a broken bridge between them. Caution signs flash and beep before ice falls on the left side or snow on the right.
- **Roadside Fights:** three floors. Cars on the road (50), birds on the sidewalk (15) and zapping power lines on top (5), each with its own caution sign and warning sound.
- **Lava Rocks:** black rocks in a lava sea. Lava burns your hp every second (jump out!), and lava geysers blast up between the rocks on a timer.

## Shop

Every fight you finish earns 10 coins, and 15 more if a person (not the computer) wins it. Spend coins in the **shop** on the main menu.

The shop is a room you walk around in. Walk your fighter up to a stand and press punch to buy what's on it, or punch again to put it on or take it off. You can also take things off from anywhere in the shop with the ✕ buttons at the bottom. To see the whole shop, close the pop-up at the bottom with its ✕ in the corner, and click **show info** to bring it back (punch still works while it's closed). Use the arrows at the top to pick which fighter you're shopping as. The shop has two floors. Downstairs is the fighty shop. Take the stairs on the left (walk up and press punch) to reach the **funny fun shop** on the 2nd floor, where things go when downstairs runs out of room. Its shopkeeper is very shy, but she'll chat if you talk to her.

A "staff only" door in the corner of the funny fun shop goes into the **break room**, where both shopkeepers take their break. The fighty shopkeeper sits on the couch with a mug of coffee and the shy shopkeeper has her tea. Talk to them and they take turns. The longer you stay, the more stuff they give you. Every 15 seconds you get coins (1 at a time at first, then more the longer you stay, up to 5). At 30 seconds the shy shopkeeper gives you a **pink bow**, at 1 minute the fighty shopkeeper gives you a **staff badge**, and at 2 minutes you get a big 20-coin bonus. The chalkboard shows your break time and what's coming next. If you leave, the timer starts over. Put the bow and badge on or take them off with the buttons at the bottom of the shop.

A ladder in the funny fun shop (with a "keep out!" sign) goes up to the dark, dusty 3rd floor, where glowing eyes watch you from the shadows and vanish when you get close. Ask either shopkeeper about the 3rd floor and they get very scared.

## Play online

1. Click **play online**, type your name and click **host**.
2. Send your friends the 4-letter code.
3. They click **play online**, type the code and click **join**.
4. Everyone picks a fighter, and the host clicks **make fight happen**.

**Public servers:** on the play online page, click **Public servers** to see five servers anyone can use, no code needed. Each one says if it's empty, how many people are waiting in the team, or if they're in the middle of a fight. Click **join** to join one, or **start it** on an empty one to become its host so other people can find you. Click **refresh** to check again.

**💬 Chat:** on an online team you can talk to each other, in the team lobby, during fights, races and horror games, and in the story together. The chat box is in the bottom left corner: press **Enter** (or click 💬) to start typing, then press Enter or **send**. Messages can be up to 60 letters long. Everyone sees your name in your fighter's color, and in fights what you said also pops up in a speech bubble over your fighter's head. While the chat is open there are quick buttons too: 👋 hi!, gg (good game!), 😂, 🔥 and 😡. Bad words turn into stars (like `****`), and you can't spam (one message a bit under every second). Old messages fade away after 12 seconds and come back when you open the chat. While you type, your fighter doesn't move, and Escape closes the chat.

The host picks the game mode in the team lobby: Free for all, Team vs team, Racing (with a track picker) or Horror.

The host's browser runs the fight, and everyone else connects to it directly using [PeerJS](https://peerjs.com/). Keep the host's game window open and in front while you play. Some school or work networks block these direct connections.

## Profiles

Everyone who plays on this computer can have their own **profile**, with their own name, picture and save: coins, chapters, shop stuff, everything. The button with your picture and name on the main menu opens the settings, where the **Profiles** cards are. Type a name and press **new profile** to make one (you start a brand new game as them). Press **play as them** to switch: the game saves the profile you're on first, then starts over as the other one. Click a picture to change it, type a name and press **rename** to rename one, and press 🗑️ to delete one (it asks first, and you can't delete the one you're playing). Your save from before profiles is the first profile, "Player 1". Up to 8 profiles.

## Your save

The game keeps your save in your browser, and nowhere else. To keep a copy, go to **settings** and press **download my save** to get a file with your chapters, coins, shop stuff and settings. If your save ever goes missing, or you move to another computer, press **load a save** and pick the file. It tells you which chapter and how many coins the file has and asks if you're sure, because it replaces the save you have now.
